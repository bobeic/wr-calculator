# ADR: Item batch 6 — every remaining damage-relevant item

**Status:** Accepted (unverified in game)
**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

36 items were hand-modelled after batch 5. The user asked for the rest, using wrpocket's latest (7.3a) numbers,
which the source-priority ADR now trusts by default. Some passives needed engine pieces that didn't exist.

## Decision

### Engine pieces

Extensions to existing kinds (all optional fields, so older data is unchanged):

- **Ratio layers everywhere.** `active`, `abilityHitProc`, `dot`, `shield` and `damageWindowProc` ratios share
  `StatRatio` (`stat`, optional `layer`, `value`), and `onHit.pctOwnStat` takes a `layer`. Needed for "bonus AD",
  "base AD" and "bonus Health" ratios (Galeforce, Goredrinker, Titanic Hydra, Dawnshroud).
- **`abilityHitProc.triggeredBy`** (`ability` by default, or `basicAttack`, `empoweredAttack`) and `oncePerCombo`:
  Duskblade, Kircheis Shard, Hextech Alternator, Titanic Hydra, Dead Man's Plate.
- **`spellblade.pctTargetMaxHp`** (Divine Sunderer) and **`extraOnHitApplications`** (Dusk and Dawn). The extra
  application rides on a new `AttackModifier.extraOnHitApplications`.
- **`procEveryN.countsFrom: 'basicAttack'`** merges the proc into the counted attack, plus `ratios`,
  `targetMissingHpAmp` (Kraken) and `startReadyInputId` (Energized attacks).
- **`active.targetMaxHpRatio`** (Redemption), **`damageAmp.scaleWithTargetBonusHp`** (Dominik's).

New kinds:

- **`attackStack`**: a self buff built by basic attacks, with `every`/`startAt` for alternating attacks and an
  optional cooldown (Phantom Dancer, Guinsoo's stacks, Terminus's Dark stacks, Yun Tal's Flurry).
- **`castBuff`**: a self buff after casting given slots, optionally limited to N attacks (Hexplate).
- **`combatAura`**: damage every N seconds while in combat; the first tick lands one interval after the first hit,
  and ticks stop once `combatWindowSeconds` pass with no other damage (Sunfire, Hollow Radiance, Bami's,
  Unending Despair). Its pending tick is a "quiet" scheduled event, so it doesn't trip the pending-damage warning.
- **`execute`** (The Collector) and **`adaptiveStat`** (Gluttonous Greaves, Immortal Boots, Spectral Sickle).

Supporting changes:

- Handlers can report **`combatStats`**: stat changes that hold right now in combat. The simulator adds them to
  the attacker's sheet at each moment, capping attack speed.
- **Stat effects honour toggle conditions** at stat resolution. Before this, a `stat` effect with a toggle applied
  whether or not the toggle was on.
- Two items need code, as `custom` handlers: Guinsoo's phantom hit and Fiendhunter's Opening Barrage.

### Data

Batch 6 lives in `packages/data/src/patches/7.3a/items-batch6.ts` and joins the 7.3a overrides. Each entry starts
from the generated 7.3a item, so price, recipe and stats stay synced to wrpocket. Only effects and stats wrpocket
doesn't list (ultimate haste, Goredrinker's omnivamp) are written by hand. It sits on 7.3a rather than the 7.3 root
because the text is 7.3a's, and putting it on the root would flag every one as stale in 7.3a.

The build-impact report lists items modelled for the first time as `newlyModelled`, not as patch changes. The stat
gold-value table only uses components with no named passive as price anchors, so modelling Tear or Recurve Bow
doesn't move stat prices.

### Left out (no effect on a 1v1 damage number)

- Holder-only defence: Guardian Angel, Zhonya's, Banshee's, Edge of Night, Verdant Barrier, Seeker's Armguard,
  Quicksilver Sash, Mercurial Scimitar, Immortal Shieldbow, Mantle, Kaenic Rookern, Bloodthirster, Death's Dance,
  Maw, Gargoyle, Randuin's, Frozen Heart, Warden's Mail, Thornmail and Bramble Vest (they need the target to attack),
  Warmog's, Spectre's Cowl, Chainlaced Crushers' shield.
- Grievous wounds (no healing modelled): Executioner's, Chempunk, Mortal Reminder, Morellonomicon, Oblivion Orb.
- Shield reduction (target shields not modelled): Serpent's Fang, Oceanid's Trident.
- Movement and utility: Youmuu's, Cosmic Drive, Rylai's, Phage, boots' movement passives, Summoner haste.
- Allies: Locket, Knight's Vow, Radiant Virtue, Mikael's, Shurelya's, Harmonic Echo, Echoes of Helia, Relic Shield.
- Runaan's Hurricane: its bolts hit other targets.

## Assumptions to check in game

Listed in `docs/test-sheets/pending-checks.md`. The ones most likely to move numbers: Energized attack cadence,
how long "in combat" lasts for auras, the Galeforce total, Guinsoo's stack duration, and Terminus's first stance.
Melee values are used where items split melee and ranged, except Yun Tal (ranged).
